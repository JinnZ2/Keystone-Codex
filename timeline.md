# Timeline

9 encoded keystones, ordered by first attestation. Candidates not yet encoded live in `data/shadow_catalogue.json`.

- **4650 BCE → 2025 CE** — **Budj Bim Eel Aquaculture (Gunditjmara)** (infrastructure, Western Victoria, Australia)  
  Stone channels, weirs, and holding ponds cut into a lava flow to divert water and hold short-finned eels (kooyang) at harvestable density year-round; hydraulic infrastructure built and maintained by people with no agriculture and no domesticated animals.

- **2600 BCE → 1700 BCE** — **Indus Valley Decentralized Plumbing & Sanitation** (infrastructure, Indus Valley)  
  House‑level drains, wells, and sewers integrated into urban grids; decentralized sanitation engineered for public health.  
  _longevity 600yr · 2 regions · decentralization 0.85_
- **1000 BCE → 1500 CE** — **Terra Preta (Amazonian Dark Earth)** (ecological, Amazon Basin)  
  Engineered, biochar‑rich soils combining charcoal, organics, and pottery shards to create long‑lived fertility and carbon sequestration.  
  _longevity 500yr · 3 regions · decentralization 0.8_
- **1200 CE → 2025 CE** — **Haudenosaunee Confederacy Councils (Kayanerenkó:wa, the Great Law of Peace)** (governance, Northeastern North America)  
  Distributed council system binding five (later six) nations under a common law, with clan mothers holding nomination and removal power over chiefs; enabled inter-nation peace and decision-making that survives the collapse of the states that surrounded it.  
  _longevity 500yr · 2 regions · decentralization 0.9_
- **1200 CE → 1600 CE** — **Quipu (Khipu)** (information, Andes)  
  Knotted‑cord information system for accounting, censuses, and narratives; decentralized, durable, low‑infrastructure data tech.  
  _longevity 300yr · 2 regions · decentralization 0.8_
- **1500 CE → 2025 CE** — **Kula Ring (Massim ceremonial exchange)** (economic, Massim archipelago, southeastern Papua New Guinea)  
  A circuit of inter-island partnerships across which two classes of valuable move in fixed opposite directions — soulava necklaces clockwise, mwali armshells counter-clockwise — neither of which is consumed, sold, or permanently held. The valuables are the ledger; the trade in food, pots, and tools moves safely alongside them.  
  _longevity 500yr · 1 regions · decentralization 0.9_
- **1500 CE → 1835 CE** — **Nunuku-whenua's Covenant (Moriori)** (ethical, Rēkohu / Chatham Islands)  
  An absolute proscription on killing, adopted after inter-group fighting on Rēkohu and enforced as law rather than urged as virtue. Disputes were settled by single combat with staves, stopped at first blood; the loser conceded and the matter ended. It held for roughly three centuries.  
  _longevity 335yr · 1 regions · decentralization 0.7_
- **1797 CE → 2025 CE** — **Precision Lathe** (material, Global (Industrial Europe origins))  
  Rotational machining enabling standardized threads, shafts, and precision parts; the mother of machine tools.  
  _longevity 200yr · 5 regions · decentralization 0.5_
- **1900 CE → 2025 CE** — **Hxaro (Ju/'hoansi delayed reciprocal exchange)** (social, Kalahari, southern Africa)  
  A network of named, lifelong partnerships across which gifts move with deliberately delayed and non-equivalent return. The goods are largely beside the point: what the exchange maintains is a standing claim on the water, food, and shelter of distant groups, callable when local conditions fail.  
  _longevity 125yr · 1 regions · decentralization 0.95_
